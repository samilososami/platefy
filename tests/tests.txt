# Platefy manual browser checklist

## Local knowledge

1. Open `/chatbot/` and confirm the welcome screen renders.
2. Enter the chat and confirm the status says `34 referencias de carta listas`.
3. Open `Carta del restaurante` and confirm the 34 items from `/menu/MENU.json` appear.

## WebLLM

1. Confirm `navigator.gpu.requestAdapter()` returns an adapter.
2. Ask: `Quiero algo sin gluten, sin frutos secos, que tenga carne y cueste menos de 14 €`.
3. Observe the one-time model download progress and then the streamed answer.
4. Expected candidates are Pollo de corral al limón and Smash Bacon only. The answer must include the allergy/cross-contamination confirmation.
5. Open Details and record response time, first token, tokens/s, estimated VRAM and JS heap delta when available.
6. Reload and ask a second question. The browser should reuse the model cache.

## Capability and failure state

1. The Thinking control is disabled because Qwen2.5 does not expose native thinking mode.
2. In a browser without a WebGPU adapter, the input must recover and show a clear compatibility message.
3. Cancelling model loading or generation must restore an editable composer without adding a stale answer.

## Responsive and voice

1. Check 390×844 and a desktop viewport for overflow, clipped controls and usable dialogs.
2. Microphone denial must leave typed chat available.
3. Sound stays opt-in. Only enabling it may send assistant text to Kokoro.
